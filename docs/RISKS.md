# Risk Register

Scale: **Likelihood** L/M/H · **Impact** L/M/H/Critical. Owner "Dev" = the solo developer; "Owner" = the product owner (user). Review at every phase gate.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **IP infringement: Picsart look-alike.** Copying Picsart branding, UI, templates, stickers or copy invites legal action | M | Critical | Original name, palette (D17), copy and illustrations. No Picsart assets ever enter the repo. Templates and stickers are self-made or CC0 with source recorded. The pre-beta review compares UI screens against Picsart for trade-dress similarity | Dev + Owner |
| R2 | **Stock licensing.** Unsplash/Pexels/Google Fonts terms are violated (missing attribution, re-hosting as a stock library, or commercial-use misstatements) | M | H | `Asset.license`, `attribution` and `sourceUrl` are required for every stock item. Follow each API's hotlinking/download-tracking rules. A "commercial use" flag is shown only when the license allows it. Quarterly license audit | Dev |
| R3 | **AI cost runaway.** Free users or abuse drive provider spend beyond budget | H | H | Credit pre-check + reserve before queueing. Per-user and per-IP rate limits (config). Hard monthly spend caps set in provider dashboards. Per-job `costUsd` logged in AIJob, with a daily cost alert. Cheap default models (FLUX schnell, Haiku-class LLM). Signup abuse controls (verified email required for AI) | Dev |
| R4 | **AI provider outage or lock-in.** A provider goes down, changes price or deprecates a model | M | M | `AIProvider` capability interfaces (D12). Second adapter (fal) planned for image tools. Model IDs live in env/config, not code. Credits are refunded automatically on provider failure. Graceful "tool temporarily unavailable" state via feature flag | Dev |
| R5 | **Unsafe content: NSFW, violence, CSAM.** Generated or uploaded content is illegal or harmful | M | Critical | Prompt moderation (LLM classifier) before job creation. Provider safety checker on every output, and flagged outputs are discarded and refunded. Uploads are private by default. **Before any public sharing ships (Phase 8): PhotoDNA/industry hash matching on uploads and outputs, NCMEC reporting procedure, report queue and takedown tooling.** AI usage policy and content policy published at beta | Dev + Owner |
| R6 | **Client-side watermark bypass** (D13). Free users strip the watermark by tampering with client code or canvas | H | L–M | Accepted for MVP: the watermark is a deterrent, not DRM. Monitor via export analytics. Server-side render for paid-quality exports (V2) moves both the watermark and the resolution cap to the server. Premium-only assets are never sent at full resolution to Free clients | Dev |
| R7 | **Canvas performance.** Interaction drops below 60 fps on large designs or low-end laptops | M | H | Konva layer caching, separate drag layer, `listening(false)` on static nodes, debounced autosave serialization. Perf test with 200 objects is an M2 exit criterion. Images downscaled to display resolution for editing, with the original used at export | Dev |
| R8 | **Konva CPU filter ceiling** (D7). Adjustments are slow on images over ~12 MP | H | M | Edit on a display-size proxy and apply the full stack only at export (in a Web Worker with OffscreenCanvas where supported). Upgrade path: a WebGL shader pass behind the same `Effect` model (V2), with no document schema change | Dev |
| R9 | **Scope creep for a solo dev.** The brief spans a product a 50-person company builds; the beta slips indefinitely | H | H | Lean cut (Phases 1–4). Phase gates with owner approval. All new ideas go to `docs/BACKLOG.md`. MoSCoW cut order in ROADMAP. Time-box each epic; a >3-day overrun triggers a cut | Owner |
| R10 | **AI output rights.** Unclear ownership or copyright of AI-generated images, and prompts that reproduce trademarks or living artists' styles | M | M | Terms state that users get the rights the provider grants, with no warranty of copyrightability. Choose providers whose model licenses permit commercial use. Block prompts that name protected marks or characters (moderation list) | Owner |
| R11 | **Data loss.** Autosave fails, conflicts overwrite work, or the DB is lost | M | Critical | Autosave every ≤ 3 s, with optimistic concurrency (`version` check; last write wins only after the user confirms). IndexedDB local snapshot for crash recovery. ProjectVersion snapshots. Neon point-in-time recovery plus a restore drill before beta. Assets are never hard-deleted synchronously | Dev |
| R12 | **Security breach** (OWASP Top 10): account takeover, IDOR on projects/assets, SSRF via URL upload, malicious uploads, XSS via SVG or fonts | M | Critical | Per-resource ownership checks in the service layer, plus tests for IDOR. Zod at every boundary. Rate limiting. URL upload fetched only by the worker, with a private-IP denylist, size/time limits and no redirects to internal hosts. MIME validated by magic bytes. SVG sanitized or rasterized. Strict CSP. Secrets only in env. Pre-beta checklist in SECURITY.md | Dev |
| R13 | **GDPR/CCPA non-compliance.** Missing consent, export, deletion or processor agreements | M | H | Cookie consent with analytics off by default in the EU. Self-serve data export and account deletion (PRD E14), with deletion completed within 30 days. DPAs signed with Vercel, Neon, Railway, Cloudflare, Replicate, Anthropic, Resend, PostHog and Sentry. Data inventory and retention table in SECURITY.md. EU region for DB/storage where possible. Privacy policy reviewed by counsel before beta | Owner |
| R14 | **DMCA / takedown handling.** No process for infringing content | L (private-only MVP) | M | DMCA page + designated agent registration before beta. Takedown tooling ships with Community (Phase 8), the point at which content becomes public | Owner |
| R15 | **Queue/worker failure.** Jobs get stuck, duplicate or are lost (worker on Railway, Redis eviction) | M | M | Idempotent jobs keyed by AIJob ID. BullMQ retries with backoff. A stalled-job sweeper refunds credits after a timeout. Redis `noeviction` policy (not Upstash eviction). Worker heartbeat on the admin health panel | Dev |
| R16 | **Vendor sprawl / free-tier limits** (Neon, Railway, Vercel hobby limits hit at beta) | M | L | Track usage monthly. Budget alerts. Everything containerized so any piece can move (Single VPS fallback) | Owner |
